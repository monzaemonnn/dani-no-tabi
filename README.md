# ダニの旅 · A dust mite's drift

A small Flower-inspired game set in a Scaniverse scan of the 1F communal floor of the Discovery Meinohama South sharehouse in Fukuoka. You are a house dust mite riding the air between lamps and AC units, waking the fabric you brush and opening the fuzz pills (毛玉) that grow on it.

Play: https://monzaemonnn.github.io/dani-no-tabi/

- Mouse to steer, W / S for pace, hold click or Space to ride the breeze. On a phone, drag to steer and hold Breeze.
- Built with three.js and three-mesh-bvh; the room model was rebuilt in Blender from the scan.
